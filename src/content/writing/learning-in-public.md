title: On Learning in Public
summary: Sharing half-formed thoughts feels risky. It's also how the best ideas grow.
date: 2026-04-10
tag: Learning
featured: true
---

There is a version of learning that stays private — notes no one reads, ideas that never leave your head. Safe, tidy, invisible.

Then there is the other kind. You write something before it's fully formed. Someone responds. The idea gets sharper, or breaks apart usefully. Either way, you learn faster than you would alone.

I'm drawn to the second kind, even when it's uncomfortable. The rough draft shared is worth more than the polished thought kept.
