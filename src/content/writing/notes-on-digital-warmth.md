---
title: Notes on Digital Warmth
summary: Warmth in interface design often comes from pacing, tone, and restraint.
date: 2025-12-12
tag: Writing
---

Warm design is not only about color. It is also about rhythm, language, and whether a page feels generous with attention.

I notice warmth when a layout gives text room to breathe, when buttons are easy to understand, and when motion is soft instead of insistent. It is a kind of hospitality.

Even small choices can make a site feel more human: softer corners, clearer labels, quieter emphasis, kinder defaults.
