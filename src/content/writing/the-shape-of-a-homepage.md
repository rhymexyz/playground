When a personal site works well, it often feels less like a pitch and more like an introduction. You arrive, you get your bearings, and you learn a little about the person behind the work.

I love homepages that leave room for breath. A clear headline, a short note of intent, a few carefully chosen links. That is usually enough.

The goal is not to say everything at once. The goal is to make someone curious enough to keep reading.
